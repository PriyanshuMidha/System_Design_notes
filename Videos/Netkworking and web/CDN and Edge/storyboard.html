Anycast

Every PoP announces the same IP address. Internet routing sends the user to the closest one.Cloudflare and Fastly work this way. Failover means a bad PoP withdraws its route.

GeoDNS

The CDN's DNS servers return a different IP per region, based on where the resolver is.CloudFront and classic Akamai setups use this. It depends on the resolver being near the user.

How do you get rid of an old copy? {1:punch}The best answer is: do not purge at all. Use versioned URLs. A new deploy means a new URL, and the old one is simply abandoned. {2}Tag purge removes only the pages tagged with one object. {3}Soft purge marks copies stale, but keeps serving them while a fresh one is fetched. {4:warn}And purge everything? Instant, but every pop hits your origin at once. That is how a routine deploy becomes an outage.
  • Never let a response with Set-Cookie be cached by a shared cache. Mark authenticated responses private or no-store.
  • Paid downloads and video: use signed URLs or signed cookies, checked at the edge.
  • Cache poisoning: an unkeyed header lets one attacker response reach every user.
  • Cache deception: adding /nonexistent.css to a private URL tricks the CDN into caching it.
Fix: the cache key must include every input the response varies on.

Then there is edge compute: your own code, running at the pop. {1}A request first passes the WAF and bot checks. {2}Then the cache. {3}And if needed, your edge function. {4}Good jobs for the edge: a JWT signature and expiry check, A B buckets, redirects, header rewrites. {5:punch}But the real authorization decision stays at the origin, because the edge only has an eventually consistent view of the world.
  • It adds a layer that can fail or serve stale data. Debugging "works on my laptop, not in Brazil" means checking a PoP you do not control.
  • It fixes aggregate traffic, not one hot key. A viral URL still funnels through one cache key.
  • Highly personalized, rarely repeated responses get little from caching. Lean on dynamic acceleration instead.
  • An internal service with no spread-out users: a plain reverse proxy or load balancer is simpler.

  • Headers decide what it may keep: max-age, s-maxage, no-cache, no-store, private.
